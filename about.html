About

Garrett Summers, 22, is a professional keyboardist based in Bozeman, Montana. He started playing piano at age two and trained in both classical and jazz piano before moving into contemporary performance.

Things He's Done

Garrett currently plays keyboard for Rob Wolf and Willy on the Wire, two acts sharing a core lineup that has played the Bandwagon Mobile Stage, opened for Eli Young, and performed at the MoJan Festival, with the CP Festival booked for summer 2027.

Before that, he founded and fronted Retrograde, releasing the debut album Speed of Sound and headlining shows to crowds in the hundreds, including a featured performance at the Gold Rush Parade. He went on to found Kerosene Record Company, working to support musicians and creative crews across the local scene.
