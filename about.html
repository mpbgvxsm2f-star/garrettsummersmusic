About

Garrett Summers

Keyboardist for Willy on the Wire and Rob Wolf. Based in Bozeman, Montana.

Rob Wolf & Willy on the Wire

Garrett plays keyboard for both Rob Wolf and Willy on the Wire, two acts that share a core lineup. Recent highlights include being the inaugural band for the Bandwagon Mobile Stage, opening for Eli Young, and playing the MoJan Festival, with the CP Festival booked for summer 2027.
