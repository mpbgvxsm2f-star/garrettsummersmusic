About

Garrett Summers

Keyboardist for Willy on the Wire and Rob Wolf. Based in Bozeman, Montana.

Early Years

Garrett started playing piano at two, by ear, and began formal lessons at four. He moved to jazz piano in sixth grade under teacher Caleb Scarguff, played in his first rock band at 12, and fronted a punk band, Break Free, at 15. He moved to Montana after high school, piano in tow.

Retrograde

A birthday-party jam session with Gus and Jack sophomore year of college turned into Retrograde. The band recorded its debut album, Speed of Sound, in an intensive stretch over spring break and the months after, released it in August 2025, and took off fast — headlining shows, opening for Pegas and The Rooftops, and playing the Gold Rush Parade with their single "Kerosene." Garrett left the band shortly after.

Kerosene Record Company

Building Retrograde into a working band meant leaning on an unpaid crew of photographers and stage hands. After the band ended, Garrett started Kerosene Record Company with Matthias Loweski to get those collaborators actually paid. It ran as an S corp for about a year, until a planned festival fell through over contract issues.

Rob Wolf & Willy on the Wire

Garrett joined Rob Wolf as keyboard player in March 2026, and Willy on the Wire shortly after — the two acts share a core lineup. Since then they've been the inaugural band for the Bandwagon Mobile Stage, opened for Eli Young, and played the MoJan Festival, with the CP Festival booked for summer 2027.
